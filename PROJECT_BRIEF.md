# Competitor — PROJECT_BRIEF.md (single source of truth, read at the start of every task)

## Product
Maths tutor for Putnam/ICMC students that never gives the solution first. It compares HOW the expert reads a problem versus HOW the student reads it, cue by cue, through one shared schema anchored to phrases of the problem text. Protocol name: ProofReader. Not a chatbot: the dashboard is a workflow document, never a message box. Never a verdict on who did better.

Successful analyses are cached per problem in localStorage (`lib/analysisCache.ts`), so revisits and resubmits skip call A for an unchanged statement. `npx tsx scripts/expert-pass.ts <dummy-id|fixture-id|file> [--grounded]` runs call A from the terminal (`--grounded` runs the production chain S→A→B→R and bundled ids get a pass/fail rubric from `scripts/rubric.ts`: kept-method route, sign-refuted dropped candidate, sign-carrying cues, answer-leak checks in symbols and in words), then a plainness report (`scripts/plainness.ts`: maths share, heavy notation, `$` in span-reason tooltips, words per field; also runs on a saved example, e.g. `npx tsx scripts/plainness.ts lib/examples/example-1.json`); `npx tsx scripts/attempt-pass.ts <fixture-id>` runs call C over git-ignored fixtures/ and reports structure, span matching, retry, duration, tokens.

## Pipeline (all live: S, A, B, C, explain)
Call S: private solve (high) + sceptical verify (medium) in `lib/providers/meta/solve.ts` — route, key steps, final line, and a small case executed in full arithmetic that the verifier recomputes from scratch; one corrected re-solve on a fail; `solution.verified` records the verdict. The solution is grounding only: carried on AnalysisSchema, cached, NEVER rendered (the product never shows a solution). Call A: expert pass (high), grounded when a verified solution exists — the kept method must be the solution's route, weights follow what it uses, simplification is its small case, nothing echoes the answer; an unverified draft is dropped silently. "The door, not the room" binds A (and R checks it): small cases must include one that breaks the naive guess (P2 keeps $n=2,4,6$, since $-2,-8$ alone pattern-match to the answer) and never sit at both ends of a "determine all" answer set; on a "prove" problem the reading says what the route establishes but leaves the decisive step as the notice_prompt question. Call B: second reading (medium, `reweighPrompt.ts`) — the complete updated chain: weights move, ≤2 spans join, second_reading records the pass; failure keeps the first reading. Call R: reading audit (medium, `auditPrompt.ts` + `audit.ts`), only when a verified solution grounds the reading — a sceptical student role-play checks the finished chain (every claim true, cues faithful to the route with conditions like signs carried in the same card, fields plain and followable — unexplained notation or notation standing in for a sentence fails, patterns stated in words are recomputed on small cases — no answer leak) and a fail buys one targeted revision; the audit's own failure keeps the unaudited chain. Call A's prompt carries two worked examples (strategy P1 + identity P2, both statements in LaTeX, answers removed, written in the house voice) and the binding writing style of `lib/providers/meta/plainStyle.ts`, shared by A, B, R's revision, explain and (as a scribe variant that quotes the student's working exactly) C: written for a student who is stuck on the problem, the statement's own notation always (never paraphrased into words), new notation only where it is shorter and clearer than the sentence it replaces and then glossed in a few words where it first appears ("$i$, the number with $i^2=-1$"), no notation standing in for reasoning, a formula over a sentence that only describes it, small worked numbers first, every technique's effect said in one clause, typically 1–3 short sentences per field (explain: diagnosis 2–3, redirection and where_to_next one sentence each), field shapes matching the UI's sentence frames (span reasons are plain-text tooltips, so no `$` there), and plain words are no loophole for leaks. Call C: student extraction from attempt pages (medium, `attemptPrompt.ts`): faithful not charitable, evidence-less stages stay empty, spans quote the statement verbatim, attempt_report records structure/inferred/missing/legibility; unguided attempts get the calm AttemptNotice. Explain call (high since 3 Nov night — at medium it repeatedly contradicted correct lines on the student's pages; `explainPrompt.ts`): writes the comparison as elaborated feedback from the solution + both chains + the authoritative TS skeleton, under binding research rules — process level never person level (Hattie & Timperley 2007; Kluger & DeNisi 1996), elaboration over verification with ≤3 ranked cards (Shute 2008), evidence quoted and located (Sadler 1989), redirection stopping short of the solution (VanLehn 2011), near-miss framed as a control decision to revisit (Schoenfeld 1985; Mueller & Dweck 1998), a self-check per card (Nicol & Macfarlane-Dick 2006). Since 3 Nov the hint ladder is binding too: the diagnosis, where_you_are and first_divergence never contain a card's move (the redirection is folded until asked for); absence claims ("no check of…") only when the student chain shows nothing; slips on the pages are called wrong wherever cited; no card repeats another; an item the chain gives no page for gets no page number. `lib/compare.ts` computes the authoritative skeleton and is the fallback when the explain call fails. Render progressively; never one long spinner. Stretch: ablation, one deciding cue removed at a time.
Honest framing the UI keeps: a grounded reading says "grounded in a route the expert solved and verified on a small case; the solution stays private"; an ungrounded one keeps "a plausible narrative, not the model's cognition". Ablation is the measured counterpart.

## Schema (shared by expert and student chains)
problem{text} — the statement exactly as typed: prose with $…$/$$…$$ maths, the single source string (write \% for a literal percent; avoid stray unpaired $). spans[{id,text,category,weight 0-100,reason}], reading{cues[{id,span_id,why_it_matters,how_spotted}],definitions[{term,meaning_latex}]}, goal{asked,answer_form,final_line_would_say}, informal_cues[{span_id,bearing}], abstraction{underneath[{object,really_is,implies}],deeper_properties[]}, simplification{instance,instance_latex,what_to_try,notice_prompt}, method{candidates[{name,deciding_span_ids,from_first_principles,kept,why}],chosen}, prerequisites[{name,needed_for,flag assumed|needed}], second_reading{new_spans,reweighted[{span_id,old,new,why}],what_changed}, domain "maths"|"not_maths".
Rules: every span is an exact substring of problem.text, checked in code — spans quote the source verbatim, so inside a formula a span is raw LaTeX (e.g. 3^0\binom{2020}{0} - …); the dashboard renders the statement as KaTeX with highlights sliced at span boundaries (a span that cuts a formula where the piece is not renderable standalone highlights the whole formula); every prose field renders $…$ inline maths; zod validates every model response; comparison is TypeScript set difference (currently hard-coded in dummy data — `lib/compare.ts` pending). The comparison is where_you_are / first_divergence / ≤3 ranked FeedbackCards (kind near_miss|wrong_turn|missed_cue|unused_mark; evidence_student quoted and page-located, expert_span_ids, diagnosis, redirection short of the solution, self_check) / unused_cues / where_to_next; student spans may be flagged `uncertain`; AnalysisSchema optionally carries attempt_report and the private solution. `/api/analyse` serves through the `lib/analysis.ts` provider seam (mock, or real `lib/providers/meta.ts` with call A wired; `AnalysisInput` lives in `lib/providers/types.ts`).

## Stack and rules
Next.js 15 App Router, TypeScript strict, Tailwind, zod, openai, katex. API routes only, no DB, no auth. Files are split by responsibility: each component does one thing, and a file is too long when it does two things, not when it passes a line count. Boring readable code; validate at the boundary; explicit loading/error states; no fetch-on-mount for user work. After every change: build, lint, verify, report.
